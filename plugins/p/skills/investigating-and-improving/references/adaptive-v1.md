# Adaptive investigation v1

This is a candidate reasoning aid, not an established productivity advantage.
Use it to choose and finish consequential work; do not create records merely to
exercise the procedure. It supplements domain methods and does not modify stored
v1 contracts. Keep checkpoints private, with safe summaries where needed.

## Choose worthwhile work

Read the request, intended use, constraints, previous decisions and observed
behavior. Resolve accessible facts yourself. Reuse supplied authority and the
original bound. Ask early only when inaccessible consequential intent or authority
would change what work is legitimate; an intake questionnaire is unnecessary.

For a broad request, inspect the relevant user path, observed failures and existing
strengths before committing to the first easy defect. Consider a credible different
opportunity and retaining the current approach. There is no idea or source quota;
a decisive mechanical repair needs no artificial brainstorm.

For live alternatives, keep enough evidence to compare:

- The affected user outcome and what currently demonstrates the problem.
- The important unknown, plausible intervention and cheapest discriminating check.
- Rough effort, potential harm and the observation that would change the choice.

Prefer a dominant option under known requirements. If ranking depends on unknown
exposure, inspect exposure where feasible; do not invent prevalence or probability.
If it depends on taste, prepare concrete alternatives and ask the appropriate
requester. Children ask their parent; a parent consolidates human-only decisions.
Continue independent work while waiting, without treating silence as permission.

Stop expanding search when remaining avenues cannot plausibly change this bounded
decision at reasonable cost. Preserve credible alternatives with specific revisit
triggers, not an open-ended backlog.

## Turn uncertainty into an outcome

Choose the next action by the decision it could change. State plausible different
observations and whether each would support implementing, revising or stopping.
Research external mechanisms when outside knowledge could change the choice;
probe when observation is more decisive. A substantial implementation can be the
right experiment. Finish its relevant verification before opening an easier branch.

Inspect the actual consumer path and contrary evidence. Separate action identity
and capture, relevance to the claim, and usefulness of the observed consequence.
For decisive commands retain exact recoverable inputs, both streams, exit and
relevant effects. Saved, hashed scripts are acceptable; include the claim-relevant
programs and data they invoke. Empty observed output differs
from missing output; nonempty output is not success. An unrelated passing check
cannot verify the change. Record changed inputs, unavailable observations and
interrupted effects explicitly; reconcile unknown effects before retrying them.
Keep secrets out of public artifacts and avoid indiscriminate environment capture.

Retain, revise, reject, keep the incumbent or leave unresolved according to the
evidence. A failed tool does not refute the idea it failed to test. Agent inspection
is not reader comprehension, preference attribution or an empirical causal effect.
Use the claim-specific domain method, including its abstention conditions.

## Progress from a result to the next action

The loop is: choose a consequential decision, investigate competing explanations,
choose an obtainable discriminating observation, execute and verify, decide what
to retain or change, then select the next action from the result. Steps can be
combined for a direct repair; this is not a required document or reply format.
Before spending on a probe, state which plausible results would change the next
choice. If none would, change the probe or reconsider whether it is needed.

After a verified result or failed attempt, revisit the enclosing objective and
remaining acceptance conditions before ending the turn. Choose explicitly:

- Continue this investigation when an important uncertainty has an affordable,
  informative next action within the remaining authority and bound. Execute it;
  a handoff saying what could happen next is not continuation.
- Revise the hypothesis or method when the result refutes it or fails to
  distinguish alternatives. A tool failure is not evidence against the hypothesis.
- Close this branch when resolved, refuted, dominated or presently infeasible.
  Reconsider the other worthwhile opportunities already found against the overall
  objective. Do not automatically choose the next easy defect or start a new study.
- Wait on a specific observation, preference or permission only for work that
  depends on it. Name its owner and resume condition; continue independent ready
  work. A due date or saved record does not schedule an agent or extend its grant.
- End the run when its actual objective is satisfied, its bound is reached, the
  requester stops it, or no useful authorized action remains after a grounded
  check. Preserve incomplete obligations and their exact prerequisites. Finishing
  a hypothesis, subtask or enabling repair does not by itself satisfy the run.

An infrastructure or measurement detour must unblock a named investigation and
have a sufficient check for returning to it. Once that check passes, resume the
blocked decision. Do not let each repaired instrument suggest another instrument
to inspect. If the original investigation is now unnecessary or infeasible, explain
why and reconsider the remaining objective instead of expanding the detour.

The result changes the route, not the user's scope, acceptance conditions or fixed
bound. Do not manufacture work to fill the time allowance or renew it when switching
branches. An exhausted weak search does not prove nothing can improve. Novel work
needs a plausible benefit and testable distinction; global optimality requires a
defined finite search or valid proof, not a plateau.

## Preserve only what continuation needs

At consequential decisions or interruption, retain purpose and original bound,
current decision, selected and parked opportunities, decisive and contrary evidence,
pending questions/effects, and the exact next action with acceptance. Link existing
artifacts rather than copying them. This is internal state, not a reply template.
Before handing off, reopen the checkpoint and check that the original bound is
actually present. For a time bound, preserve the absolute deadline established at
intake, not just “three minutes” or a fresh duration. If the start was not observed,
record that limitation rather than inventing it. Keep a pending question pending
even when independent work is complete; name the evidence needed to resume it.
Before declaring the time bound exhausted, compare an observed current clock with
that deadline. If the clock is unavailable, remaining time is unknown, not zero.
A permission blocker does not exhaust time or justify asking to renew the bound.
Recover those facts before repeating work. Native tool execution and actual effects
remain authoritative; plain notes do not provide transactional enforcement or a
legacy completion audit. Delegate only when authorized, without renewing bounds.

For self-improvement, freeze the incumbent and evaluator before confirmation on
subsequent work. Count omissions, failures, exclusions and measurement burden.
An improvement to this demonstration is not evidence of better future investigations.
Retain optional status when comparative benefit is unresolved; removal of costly
unsupported guidance is a valid candidate improvement.
