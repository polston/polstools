# Empirical comparison

Identify the sampling unit, estimand, eligibility, assignment, comparator,
confounders, protected quality conditions and missingness before interpreting
an effect. Repeated sessions or variants of one source are not automatically
independent units. Use the existing workflow pilot/comparison machinery when its
assumptions fit; its descriptive reports cannot manufacture causal evidence.

Compare candidate and incumbent with comparable information and resources.
Register practical benefit and selection rules before confirmation. Choose sample
coverage from development variability and the decision at stake; do not invent a
universal sample size. Small convenience samples support descriptive findings.
Non-significance does not prove equivalence or no harm.

Record all assigned cases, including failed and incomplete attempts. Separate
candidate changes from evaluator changes and regrade both arms when grading is
corrected. Retain exposure history; repeatedly consulted tests become selection
evidence. Quantitative outcomes need denominators and uncertainty; qualitative
assessments need inspectable examples and attributed judgment.

For interrupted-work studies, use the recovery evaluation helpers documented in
`<plugin-root>/INVESTIGATION.md` when their contract fits. Keep actual requested
outcomes, worker-visible knowledge, safe retry, conservative allowance, report
units and administrative closure separate. Bind service-specific normalization
to exact artifacts; a populated report or ready audit is not outcome evidence.
Require verified prior-writer cleanup before recovery and retain telemetry
missingness. Freeze the evaluator and adapter before scoring; preserve and
symmetrically reassess both arms when either changes. Portable rules do not
establish that a native harness adapter was exercised live.
